# About This Chatbot

This Symptom Information Chatbot is designed for **educational purposes only**.  

### Features:
- Provides **general guidance about symptoms**.
- Offers **tips for common conditions**.
- Suggests **when to consult a healthcare professional**.
- Does **not replace professional medical advice or diagnosis**.

### How to Use:
1. Describe your symptoms in the text input field.
2. Click **Get Info**.
3. The chatbot will return information related to your symptoms.
4. Always consult a **licensed healthcare provider** for accurate diagnosis and treatment.

**Disclaimer:** The chatbot is for **informational purposes only**. It cannot diagnose or treat medical conditions.
