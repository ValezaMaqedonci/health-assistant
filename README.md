# 🩺 Smart Health Assistant

**Welcome to the Smart Health Assistant!**

This web-based AI health assistant is designed to help users learn about their symptoms, summarize health articles, identify medicines from images, and track daily health habits. It’s educational and informative — **not a substitute for professional medical advice**.

## Features

1. **Symptom Info Chatbot**
    * Ask about your symptoms and get helpful, general guidance.
    * Provides tips and suggestions for common issues like headaches, coughs, or fever.
    * Includes a friendly doctor character, **Dr. SHADOWS**, for a welcoming experience.

2. **Medicine Image Identifier**
    * Upload images of medicine packages and extract readable text using OCR.
    * Useful for tracking or identifying medications.

3. **Daily Health Tracker**
    * Log steps, sleep hours, water intake, and mood.
    * View weekly trends, track streaks, earn badges, and get tips.
    * Features a walking character to encourage activity.

4. **Settings**
    * Change themes in black or white color
    * Tell your honest feedback
    * Leave your review for this chatbot

5. **Daily Quiz**
    * Quizes your mind about health trivia
    * More than 3 questions total
    * New questions everytime you refresh
    * New fact every time you refresh

**Disclamer**
* This chatbot is for educational reasons only and not meant to cure or diagnose diseases
